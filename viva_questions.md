# 35 Viva Questions
1 What is DBMS? Software that manages stored data and controlled access.
2 Why DBMS? Reduces redundancy and improves integrity, concurrency and recovery.
3 Primary key? Unique minimal row identifier.
4 Foreign key? Reference to another relation's key.
5 Normalization? Reduces redundancy/anomalies.
6 1NF? Atomic values.
7 2NF? No partial dependency.
8 3NF? No transitive dependency.
9 BCNF? Every determinant is a candidate key.
10 4NF? Removes non-trivial multivalued dependencies.
11 Relational algebra? Procedural operations on relations.
12 Selection? Row filtering.
13 Projection? Column selection.
14 Join? Combines related rows.
15 Division? “For all” queries.
16 View? Virtual table from a query.
17 Index? Access structure that speeds lookup.
18 B+ tree? Balanced ordered index structure.
19 Hashing? Key-to-bucket mapping.
20 Trigger? Automatically executed database event logic.
21 Why medical procedure too? Time passing alone does not fire row triggers.
22 Stored procedure? Named database program.
23 Transaction? Logical unit of work.
24 ACID? Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability.
25 COMMIT? Makes transaction changes permanent.
26 ROLLBACK? Undoes uncommitted changes.
27 SAVEPOINT? Partial rollback marker.
28 Serializability? Concurrent schedule equivalent to serial execution.
29 Shared vs exclusive lock? Shared for read; exclusive for write.
30 Deadlock? Circular waiting among transactions.
31 Timestamp protocol? Orders conflicting operations by timestamps.
32 2PC? Prepare/vote then commit/abort for distributed atomicity.
33 Recovery? Restores consistent state after failures.
34 Why trigger foster capacity? Enforces business rule at DB level.
35 Why Flask? Provides a simple functional interface to the relational database.
